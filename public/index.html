Is your wallet ready for Q-Day?

Every ordinary Solana address is its public key, in plain view. A powerful enough quantum computer could one day work backwards from it to the secret key. Paste any address and see what sits behind it.

How it works, simply

Crypto wallets are locks. Today's locks are excellent against today's computers. A different kind of machine changes the arithmetic.

i.

Your address is a lock everyone can see

Your private key is the combination. Today's computers would need billions of years to work it out from the lock alone. That's why crypto works.

ii.

Quantum computers are good at exactly that puzzle

None is strong enough yet, and nobody knows when one will be: maybe years, maybe never. The day it happens is called Q-Day.

iii.

A different kind of lock fixes it

Hash-based locks rely on maths quantum computers are bad at. Each combination works once, so funds move to a fresh lock every time they're spent.

Reading the result

"Exposed" describes readiness, not danger today. No quantum computer can do this yet, and Solana has a plan to move to post-quantum signatures when it's needed.
